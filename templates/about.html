About Us

Empowering Healthy Choices with AI – Ensuring Quality & Nutrition in Every Bite!

We are final-year students at the Faculty of Computers and Information, Ain Shams University, specializing in Scientific Computing. Our passion for technology and innovation has driven us to develop an AI-powered system that enhances food safety and nutritional awareness.

Food quality and calorie estimation play a crucial role in health and well-being. With the rise of processed foods and changing dietary habits, ensuring the freshness and nutritional value of what we consume has never been more important. Our project leverages artificial intelligence and computer vision to analyze food quality and estimate calorie content, providing users with valuable insights to make healthier choices. By integrating technology into daily life, we strive to contribute to a future where food safety and nutrition are more accessible, helping individuals lead healthier and more informed lifestyles
